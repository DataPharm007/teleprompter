Scrolling

Off: the script stays put and you drive it — arrow keys, PgUp/PgDn, a clicker, the mouse wheel or a drag. Voice tracking moves it regardless of this setting.

Countdowns

Lead-in runs before the scroll starts; the hold keeps the camera rolling after the last line.

Voice activation

Voice commands: start / go, stop / pause, faster / speed up, slower, bigger, smaller, back to the top, mirror. In Follow + commands say the command on its own, with a short break either side — that is how it tells an instruction apart from the words of your script.

Keyboard & remotes

Most Bluetooth presenter clickers send PgUp/PgDn or arrows, so they work as remotes with no setup.
